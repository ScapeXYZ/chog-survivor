# Chog Survivor character acceptance checklist

Use this checklist before replacing the current Home character. A candidate
must pass every required item. Passing does not grant Chog Genesis branding or
commercial usage rights.

## Visual identity — required

- [ ] Full-body, compact creature reads at 256 px tall without relying on a background or caption.
- [ ] Rounded head measures 50–60% of total standing character height.
- [ ] Crown contains clearly overlapping, swept-back leaf-shaped spikes with consistent root direction and tapered forms.
- [ ] Eyes are large, circular, deliberately outlined, matched in construction, and have restrained icy-blue highlights.
- [ ] Face has a small nose and readable determined/alert mouth; it is neither animal-photoreal nor humanoid.
- [ ] Body has short arms, short legs, four coherent rounded paws, and a stable three-quarter stance.
- [ ] The silhouette is distinct, balanced, and recognizable without armour, weapons, clothing, hair, cape, scarf, jewellery, or props.
- [ ] No extra/missing limbs, distorted eyes, melted features, arbitrary spikes, asymmetrical facial errors, or generic fantasy-creature substitutions are visible.

## Illustration and lighting — required

- [ ] Linework is clean, consistent, and intentionally 2D illustrated.
- [ ] Smooth blue-grey palette, controlled cel shading, and subtle texture are coherent across head, spikes, torso, and paws.
- [ ] Upper-left moonlight, blue rim light, and lower-right restrained crimson bounce are consistent with the Home scene.
- [ ] No excessive glow, plastic skin, realistic fur, photographic anatomy, pasted-on lighting, repeated texture patterns, or visible generation artifacts.
- [ ] Feet can plausibly meet the Home terrain after placement; no baked floor, plinth, rubble, or drop shadow is present.

## Technical delivery — required

- [ ] PNG source is RGBA and at least 2048 px tall; final canvas has the requested transparent safety margins.
- [ ] A lossless PNG source and optimized transparent WebP derivative are supplied with the specified provisional filenames.
- [ ] Alpha edges are clean against black, white, and the moonlit Home background at 1× and 0.5× scale.
- [ ] No environment, text, logo, watermark, UI, or baked-in shadow is present.
- [ ] At the Home target box (about 25–30% of desktop scene width), the eyes, spike silhouette, and face remain readable without covering the moon, gateway, or left text.
- [ ] Mobile crop retains head, face, and principal spikes without stretching or horizontal overflow.

## Rights and release control — required

- [ ] Asset inventory records filename, creator/source, master location, dimensions, alpha format, and local/release approval state.
- [ ] A written license or permission grant covers the intended use. Public NFT availability alone is not accepted as a license.
- [ ] The asset remains marked provisional until human visual approval and Chog branding/IP permission are both recorded.
