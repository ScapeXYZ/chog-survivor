# Judge testing guide

Play: https://chog-survivor.vercel.app/ · [Guest Mode](https://chog-survivor.vercel.app/#guest) · [Narrated 60-second walkthrough](https://chog-survivor.vercel.app/chog-survivor-narrated-60s.mp4).

## No wallet or NFT — about one minute

1. Open Guest Mode. Expect a Chog character, Broken Bridge and three choices immediately.
2. Pick a choice. Expect its consequence and updated **guest** XP, Trust and Scars. Try different trade-offs through six encounters.
3. At the end, a silver glimmer appears in 1 in 10 journeys. If present, follow it for The Moonlit Seed: shelter the sprite, study the seed or seize its light. Each choice changes guest stats only. Its absence is normal; no purchase or wallet can unlock it.
4. Restart to compare paths. Expect a clean guest journey. Guest progress is temporary, off-chain and never an NFT record, transaction or earned mainnet badge.

## Genuine Chog Genesis holder — reads and review

1. Choose **Play with Your NFT** and explicitly connect MetaMask, Rabby or another discovered compatible EVM wallet. Select Monad mainnet, chain **143**. Never share a secret or bypass a wallet warning.
2. Select an owned Chog from discovery, or enter its token ID manually. Expect live `ownerOf` matching the wallet before actions. A non-owner, wrong network, missing token or unavailable RPC cannot unlock gameplay. Failed reads show loading/error/unavailable states.
3. Character shows genuine XP, Trust, Scars, recorded streak, title and earned achievements. A never-played token can legitimately have zero progression. Records follow the token when ownership transfers, including its daily lock.
4. Adventure offers the contract’s current encounter. Choose a decision to inspect the **unsigned** review: exact token/action, zero MON value, gas and maximum cost. **Cancel here for a transaction-free judging session.** A completed daily action stays locked until the next UTC day.
5. A holder who independently elects to play must inspect and explicitly approve both app review and native wallet confirmation. Gas costs real MON. Limits remain 400,000 gas, 125 gwei total gas price, 0.05 MON estimated maximum cost and 0.1 MON remaining reserve. No transaction is required to judge this project.

Success is shown only after verified receipt/event/state checks. Rejection or failed preflight must not claim success. Pending/uncertain transactions must not be blindly retried. The contract is immutable and unaudited; the video contains no completed mainnet action or invented receipt.

## Why return?

Each token gets one decision per UTC day. Consecutive actions build streaks and add prior-streak XP up to +7; missing a day restarts the next action’s streak at 1. Seven Suns marks 7 consecutive actions; Unmarked Week requires 7 consecutive actions requesting no extra Scars. At 20 prior Scars, cautious/reckless choices change scar requests. These are existing contract mechanics, not payouts. Open **The next dawn** in Adventure for details and verified milestone status when available. Encounters may repeat; new content and additional rewards are roadmap ideas.
